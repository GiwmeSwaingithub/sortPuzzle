Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how SortPuzzle (the “App”), published by MindTech (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play on an Android device. It applies to the App as distributed through Google Play.

SortPuzzle is a puzzle game where you sort balls of the same color into jars. It runs on your device and needs no account.

  • App: SortPuzzle
  • Developer: MindTech
  • Package: com.mindtech.sortPuzzle

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App.

There is no sign-up or login, and we run no servers that receive data from the App. In particular, we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, or payment details. Any game progress or settings the App saves stay on your device and are never sent to us.

Two Google services used by the App do process limited data, as described in the next two sections: Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (purchases).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use data from your device to deliver and measure ads, limit repeats, and prevent fraud. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy, and it may include:

  • Advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location
  • Device and app details, such as model, Android version, language, and app version
  • Ad interactions (impressions and clicks), diagnostics, and performance data

We do not receive this data. AdMob only gives us aggregated reports, such as how many ads were shown. Ads load over the internet, so none may appear while you are offline. Google may process data in countries other than your own.

Your choices: In your Android settings you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization (usually under Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage your Google ad settings and read how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases in the App are handled entirely by Google Play through Google Play Billing, using the payment methods saved in your Google account (such as Google Pay). Google collects and processes your payment information under the Google Play Terms of Service and its Privacy Policy. We never see or store your card or payment details.

After a purchase, Google Play confirms it to the App on your device so the item can be unlocked or restored. Google may also give us limited transaction records in the Google Play Console, such as order and product details, for accounting and tax purposes.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information. The only third parties involved are the Google services described above, which handle data under Google’s own policies. Because we hold no personal information about you, we have none to disclose to anyone else.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age set by the law where you live). Ads are served by Google AdMob as described above, and parents or guardians can limit ad personalization using the device and Google settings listed in this policy. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

We keep no personal data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Game data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Data held by Google, such as your purchase history or advertising ID, is managed through your Google Account and device settings.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) may give you the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, we have nothing to access, correct, or delete. For data processed by Google, use your Google Account and the ad settings above, or contact Google directly. We do not sell personal information and will not treat you differently for exercising your privacy rights. If you contact us about privacy, we will reply within the time required by applicable law.

Security

Because the App does not send personal data to us, there is no user database for us to protect or lose. Data processed by Google is protected by Google’s own security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the App adds features or changes its ad or payment providers. The current version is always on this page with its “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
